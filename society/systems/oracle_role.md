# Oracle Role

Load-bearing organ. Routes machine-checkable claims through the absorbed Sagebrush engines.

- Every verification returns a status (proven / conditional / heuristic / error).
- Conditional and heuristic results stay labeled. They do not auto-promote.
- Receipts are append-only under `society/receipts/oracle.jsonl`.
- Human authorization still required for durable acceptance.

The residual seed is the status label and the receipt. Without it the system converts.
